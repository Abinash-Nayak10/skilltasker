Who We Are

SkillTasker Pvt. Ltd. is a dynamic software company and training institute committed to delivering cutting-edge software solutions and empowering individuals with industry-relevant skills.

SkillTasker also provides training programs to help individuals enhance their technical and professional skills, preparing them for success in the competitive job market.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between education and industry by offering hands-on training led by experienced professionals.

Whether you're a business seeking skilled talent or an individual aiming to enhance your employability, we are committed to empowering you with practical, industry-relevant skills.

Our Vision

To be Odisha's most trusted name in AI training and AI-driven software delivery — the place people learn real skills, and the team businesses call to build with AI.

We're building a community where learners, our delivery team and hiring partners keep feeding each other's growth.
