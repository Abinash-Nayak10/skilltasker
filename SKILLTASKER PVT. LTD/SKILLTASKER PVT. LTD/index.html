Who we are

Skills shape the future — we build both the people and the products.

SkillTasker Pvt. Ltd. is an Odisha-based training and technology company. We run hands-on, mentor-led programs in web development, app development, data analysis and applied AI — and we run a development studio that ships websites, apps and AI automation for real businesses.

That combination is deliberate: our trainers are practitioners who build client projects, and our client work is powered by the same curriculum we teach. It's why learners leave with portfolio-ready work, and why our AI solutions are grounded in production experience, not slideware.

  • Practical, project-based curriculum — not just theory
  • Trainers who are active industry practitioners
  • In-house AI & software delivery team for businesses
  • Internship and placement support for learners
